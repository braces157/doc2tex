# Community guidelines

Help make this project useful and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Be respectful and specific when giving feedback.
- Discuss ideas and behavior without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy. Do not post private documents, credentials, contact information, or identifying metadata without permission.
- Do not harass, discriminate against, threaten, or impersonate others.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and limit participation when needed. Repeated or serious misconduct may result in a ban from project spaces.

For a problem that should not be public, use GitHub's private reporting or abuse-reporting tools. For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include private information in a public issue to report someone else's behavior.
